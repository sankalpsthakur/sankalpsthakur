# Upstream contributions

The badge counts [merged PRs in public upstream repositories](https://github.com/search?q=is%3Apr+is%3Apublic+author%3Asankalpsthakur+is%3Amerged+-user%3Asankalpsthakur&type=pullrequests), plus verified credited landings and explicitly acknowledged incorporations from closed PRs. Personal and private repositories are excluded; replacement PRs are never counted twice.

It updates automatically every six hours. Discovery follows direct commits, linked replacement PRs and cross-repository migrations, checking GitHub-linked authorship/coauthorship or an upstream author's explicit statement that the original changes were incorporated. Every landing is verified on the upstream default branch. Related references, independent alternatives, promises of credit and unmerged replacements do not qualify. There is no hand-maintained list.

[Latest count and evidence](https://gist.github.com/sankalpsthakur/9cded476f436ff505a842e1f9293c4ae) · [Counting code](./scripts/count_upstream.py)

Incomplete or failed scans keep the previous badge. Evidence records the update time, commits, replacement URLs and separate totals for merged PRs, authorship-credited landings and acknowledged incorporations. Acknowledgment is not represented as Git authorship. This is a historical contribution count; it does not claim every change remains unreverted.
